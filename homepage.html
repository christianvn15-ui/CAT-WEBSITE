The Impact of Technology on the Online Sales Industry in South Africa

Focus Question

What is the impact of technology on the online sales industry for companies in South Africa?

Abstract

This investigation explores how digital tools such as artificial intelligence, mobile applications, and real-time tracking systems are transforming the online sales industry in South Africa. It examines the benefits and challenges of these technologies, focusing on their role in improving efficiency and expanding digital presence. The study is designed for educators, learners, and businesses seeking insight into the evolving e-commerce landscape, with findings aligned to PAT requirements.
